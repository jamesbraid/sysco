# Fixed limits of the L2, the L1 and the boot firmware that bound a 512-processor system

Companion to [capacity-limits.md](capacity-limits.md), which holds the 10-record L1 module table, the 40 ports per L2 node, the 1024-word brick list, the 64-partition database, the rack and slot ranges and the power-up slot lists. This note lists every other fixed limit found in a static pass, says what each bounds and what happens past it, and says whether the largest published configuration reaches it. Written 2026-11-13.

Images: L2 1.44.0 (`l2.bin` of IRIX 6.5.30, sha256 `6bcfda07a24cb4d56850922dab17a98223abcd14221d307e3a8c3778434e8761`; carved and listed as in [image-layout.md](image-layout.md); executables load at `0x10000000`, libraries at 0). L1 limits marked "from the notes" were not read again here; they are taken from [../l1/usb-bulk.md](../l1/usb-bulk.md), [../l1/chunked-request-frames.md](../l1/chunked-request-frames.md) and [../l1/rs422-router.md](../l1/rs422-router.md) with those notes' own grades. The boot firmware is the IP35 PROM image of IRIX 6.5.30 (SGI Version 6.210, built 26 August 2004); only strings and the message set were read in it. Grades as in [../../AGENTS.md](../../../AGENTS.md).

The largest published configuration is the Origin 3800 at 512 processors: 128 four-processor C-bricks, at most 8 C-bricks per compute rack, at most 16 compute racks, at most 8 I/O bricks per I/O rack and no cap on I/O racks in the manual, one L2 per compute rack (publication 108-0266-001, appendix D and sections 3.18.2 to 3.18.4). Each compute rack has 1 to 3 R-bricks: appendix D gives three as the maximum, section 7.2.2 shows one or two, and the 512-processor drawing has 44 R-bricks in total, not the 48 that three per rack would give. Every "512p" verdict below is judged against that.

## Result for 512 processors

No fixed table is reached by the largest published configuration. Per L2 the load is at most 8 C-bricks and 3 R-bricks (at most 11 L1s, 10 by the placement guidance) against 40 ports and 400 records; the partition scheme's 6-bit rack field has 64 values, which covers 16 compute racks and the I/O racks (the 32-and-32 split of the rack code is not relied on); slots stay inside a 39-unit rack. The three places where size changes behaviour without a hard stop are the single reply deadline per batch (row 3), the flash rewrite per event line (row 2) and the PROM's per-request waits (row 16).
